Official guild instructions for apparatus physics, dissection grades, and covert trade.

I. The Long Drop Mechanics & Cervical Energy

The long drop is designed to fracture the second and third cervical vertebrae cleanly, severing the spinal cord. It is neither slow asphyxiation nor violent decapitation.

Kinetic Goal: Ideal Impact Energy = 1,180 to 1,260 ft-lbs (modified by neck rigidity traits).

Formula: Drop Length (Inches) = [Target Energy / Body Weight (lbs)] × 12

Example: A 160 lb subject with standard resistance requires: (1,200 / 160) × 12 = 90 inches (7'6").

  • Variance < -15% (Too Short): Insufficient energy. The subject convulses and strangles over 10–15 minutes. Heavy penalty to Public Order.
  • Variance > +15% (Too Long): Excessive kinetic force tears cervical tissue, causing catastrophic decapitation and crowd hysteria. Heavy Crown fine.
  • Ideal Zone (±6%): Instantaneous humane fracture. High Crown Favor and high dissection purity.

II. Halifax Guillotine & Cleaving Power

The guillotine converts gravitational potential into pure shear cleaving momentum. Friction along the timber stiles is the primary source of failure.

Kinetic Energy: Joules = Mass (kg) × Height (m) × 9.8 × (Grease Lubrication % / 100)

Cleave Threshold: Must cleanly balance [Neck Rigidity × 8.0 + (Weight × 0.4)].

III. The Mortuary Harvest & Purity Ratings

Once dispatched, remains are moved to the cold slabs. Relic quality and organ preservation directly reflect the sentence's precision:

Pristine (90-100%): Flawless snap. Specimen organs intact. 100% market value.
Torn (50-80%): Minor trauma or delay. Relics yield ~70% base auction value.
Ruined (<50%): Violent decapitation or bludgeoning. Reduced to base tallow.
